Gold layer · dimensional model

Index vs Trust Schema

Star schema comparing S&P 500 total return against a performance-ranked basket of UK investment trusts, plus the manager-tenure dimension that carries the project's slowly-changing history.

5 tables · 2 fact tables · 3 dimensions · 1 SCD2 dimension

Why two stars

The model isn't a single star — it's a small constellation. fact_monthly_performance answers the headline question (index vs. trust returns, month by month), while fact_manager_tenure_performance answers a narrower one (did performance change when the manager changed). Both facts share dim_financial_instrument as the bridge, so the two questions stay joinable without forcing everything into one oversized fact table.

Dimension Fact table

Versioning rule

A new dim_manager row opens whenever either manager_name or management_group changes — a firm handover and a named-manager handover don't always land on the same date. Keeping both attributes versioned in one table means there's only ever one row that's "current" for a trust at a given date, instead of two SCD2 tables with dates to reconcile.
